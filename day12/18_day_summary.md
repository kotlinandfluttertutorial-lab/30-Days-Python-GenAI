# Day 12 — Day Summary: Deep Learning Engineering

## What You Covered
CNN for local pattern detection, RNN/LSTM for sequential dependencies, transfer learning with BERT, BatchNorm vs LayerNorm, early stopping, LR scheduling.

## Key Takeaways
1. CNN detects local patterns (n-grams in text); LSTM models sequential context
2. Transfer learning: always start from pretrained, fine-tune rather than train from scratch
3. Freeze then unfreeze: prevents catastrophic forgetting during fine-tuning
4. BatchNorm after linear layers; LayerNorm in Transformers
5. Warmup scheduler: standard for fine-tuning pretrained models
