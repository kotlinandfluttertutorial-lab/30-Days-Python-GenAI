## What is NotebookLM?

Google NotebookLM is an AI-powered notebook that can read your documents and answer questions, generate quizzes, create summaries, and explain concepts based on the content you upload. This program is specifically structured for NotebookLM import.

## Daily NotebookLM Workflow

### Before studying each day:
1. Open the relevant notebook
2. Ask: "What should I know before studying [today's topic]?"
3. Ask: "What are the key concepts for [today's topic]?"

### During study:
1. After reading `02_concepts.md`, ask NotebookLM to quiz you
2. After coding, ask: "What mistakes do beginners make with [concept]?"

### After each day:
1. Import the day's `17_notebooklm_notes.md`
2. Ask: "Create 5 flashcards for today's key concepts"
3. Ask: "How does today's topic connect to [previous topic]?"

### Before interviews:
1. Open Notebook 7 (Interview Master)
2. Ask: "Simulate a 30-minute technical interview"
3. Ask: "What are the 3 most important things I need to know about [topic]?"
