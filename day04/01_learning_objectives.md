## What You Will Accomplish Today

By end of Day 4, you will have the mathematical intuition that explains why every AI algorithm works the way it does.

### Knowledge Objectives
1. Understand scalars, vectors, matrices as data representations
2. Compute dot products, norms, and distances
3. Understand probability and conditional probability
4. Apply Bayes' theorem conceptually to classification
5. Understand mean, variance, standard deviation — and why they matter
6. Implement cosine similarity from first principles
7. Understand why cosine similarity is used for embeddings (not Euclidean)

### Skill Objectives
1. Implement all operations from scratch (no NumPy shortcuts)
2. Implement the same operations efficiently with NumPy
3. Build a `SimilarityCalculator` mini-project
4. Compute distances between embeddings

### Project: Similarity Calculator

## Why This Day Matters

Every AI operation maps to math:
- Embedding search = cosine similarity (vectors)
- Neural network training = gradient descent (calculus + linear algebra)
- Bayesian classification = conditional probability
- Feature normalization = mean/std (statistics)

You don't need to derive proofs. You need to know: **what does this math do and why is it the right tool?**
