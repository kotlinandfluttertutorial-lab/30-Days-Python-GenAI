# Day 04 — Day Summary: AI Mathematics

## What You Covered
The mathematical foundations behind every AI operation: vector similarity (embeddings), softmax (LLM token selection), statistics (evaluation), attention math (transformers).

## Key Takeaways
1. Cosine similarity = direction, not magnitude → correct for text
2. Softmax converts logits to probabilities; temperature controls sharpness
3. Standard deviation tells you how spread evaluation scores are
4. Attention = weighted combination of values based on query-key similarity
5. Embedding algebra: semantic relationships are geometric transformations
